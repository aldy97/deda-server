# Unlock L1 Speaking & Listening

## unit-listening-1: Seasons and Weather

Key vocabulary:

- cold (adj): having a low temperature
- hot (adj): having a high temperature
- snow (n): soft, white pieces of frozen water which fall from the sky
- spring (n): the season of the year between winter and summer
- autumn (n): the season of the year between summer and winter
- temperature (n): how hot or cold something is
- weather (n): the temperature or conditions outside

Example sentences:

- Dubai's hot in July.
- Canada has a lot of snow in winter.
- We get snow when it's cold here.
- Take a look at the photos.
- They are all from one place.
- There's a beautiful beach next to a big lake.
- It's winter and there's a mountain.
- It's hot and there's sand.

Discussion questions:

- Describe the weather in your country.
- What is your favourite season?
- Which photo shows hot weather?
- Which photo shows cold weather?

## unit-listening-2: Famous People and Jobs

Key vocabulary:

- businessman (n)
- businesswoman (n)
- chef (n)
- scientist (n)
- teacher (n)
- writer (n)
- creative (adj): good at thinking of new ideas or using imagination
- free (adj): not costing any money

Famous people in this unit:

- Nadiya Hussain: a chef and a TV presenter, writes about food
- Larry Page: Co-founder of Google
- Ursula Burns: former CEO of Xerox
- Salman Khan: founder of Khan Academy, education for all

Example sentences:

- Nadiya Hussain writes about food.
- Larry Page is the Co-founder of Google.
- Ursula Burns is the former CEO of Xerox.
- Nadiya is very creative.
- Nadiya and her husband have three children.
- Salman is from California.
- Salman's wife is a doctor.

Taking notes:
When you listen to a presentation or talk, it's a good idea to take notes. This helps you remember the information later. When you take notes, you write down the important information you hear. You do not need to write complete sentences.

Discussion questions:

- What do you think is the best job in the world?
- What do you think is the worst job?
- Which of the jobs do you want to do? Why?

## unit-speaking-task: Tell Your Group About a Famous Person

How to introduce and start a talk:

- I'm going to tell you about a famous person from [country].
- This is [name].
- He / She's a famous [nationality] [job].

What to talk about:

- Introduce your person.
- Show your photo.
- Talk about your person's job, family and home.
- Talk about why your person is famous.

Example sentences:

- I'm going to tell you about two famous people from Egypt.
- Karim Abdel Aziz is a famous actor.
- Carmen Suleiman's a famous singer.
- Karim's father is Mohammed Abdel Aziz.
- He's a film director.
- Karim's aunt is Samira Muhsin.
- She's an actor.

Useful questions:

- Who's the person in your ideas map?
- Who is in his / her family?
- Where's he / she from?
- What is his / her job?
- Why is he / she famous?

Task checklist:

- Use an ideas map.
- Talk about a famous person from your country.
- Use the verb be, possessive adjectives and subject pronouns correctly.
- Use syllable stress correctly.
