# Sample English Starter (Markdown)

## unit-1: Greetings

Key sentences:
- Hello! / Hi!
- How are you?
- I'm fine, thank you.
- Nice to meet you.
- Good morning. Good afternoon. Good evening.

Dialogue:
A: Hello, I'm Amy. What's your name?
B: Hi Amy, I'm Tom. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too. How are you today?
B: I'm fine, thank you. And you?
A: I'm great! Let's learn English together.

## unit-2: Colors and Shapes

Key words:
Colors: red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, black, white.
Shapes: circle, square, triangle, rectangle, star.

Key sentences:
- What color is it?
- It's red.
- What shape is it?
- It's a circle.

Dialogue:
A: Look at the sky. What color is it?
B: It's blue.
A: And the sun?
B: The sun is yellow and round like a circle.
A: Great job! Can you find something green?
B: Yes, the grass is green.
